Pipeline step 4

Data Cleaning — fix, flag, never delete blindly

Every transformation writes to a global audit log: table, operation, rows before/after, motivation.
  • Semantic nulls ≠ missing: undelivered orders keep NULL dates — flagged, never imputed
  • Imputation: category → "unknown" (MCAR); physical measures → median, robust to outliers
  • IQR outliers only flagged: 8,427 on price — original values preserved
  • Geolocation: 1,000,163 rows → 19,015 one-centroid-per-ZIP records
  • Known limit documented: 4-digit ZIPs left as-is — harmless at city/state grain

The Reviews case — 814 records saved

The scorecard assumed review_id as PK and reported duplicates. The real natural key is the pair (review_id, order_id): deduplicating on the single column would have silently deleted 814 legitimate records.

Measured downstream impact

690 orders recovered their rating in fact_sales — 549 review scores went from NULL to a real value (non-null: 111,159 → 111,708 of 112,650).

Pipeline step 5 — DFM

Design Decisions

Data-driven method: attribute tree from functional dependencies, then pruning and grafting.

01 · Granularity

The fact is the single order item — the finest grain available. Per-product and per-seller analysis stays possible; rolling up to the order is always possible, the reverse is not.

02 · Pruning & grafting

Pruned customer_unique_id, free-text reviews, audit flags. Grafted zip_code_prefix: city and state connect directly to customer/seller.

03 · Multiple payments

One order, many instalments → aggregated per order (SUM of values, method of the first instalment). Declared future step: a bridge table.

04 · Additivity, declared measure by measure

payment_value is replicated on every item row: SUM at item level overstates — R$ 19.2M vs 15.4M real. Additive (price, freight) · order-level additive (payment) · semi-additive (delays: AVG/MIN/MAX) · non-additive (review score: AVG only).

WHY STAR

Fewer joins, faster OLAP aggregations; short hierarchies (city → state) denormalised inside the dimensions. The only 3 NULL FKs: one delivered order with no payment recorded in the source — a known dataset quirk, not an ETL error.

Results

Key Findings & Conclusions

  • Quality in, quality out: measured DQ before and after — the scorecard, not intuition, drove every fix
  • One wrong key can delete correct data: the Reviews composite key saved 814 records and 549 ratings
  • Additivity must be declared: or dashboards silently overstate (R$ 19.2M vs 15.4M)
  • Business answers: health_beauty leads revenue · SP dominates volume · deliveries early on average but the North waits 3× longer · Black Friday '17 peak
  • Future work: bridge table for multiple payments · macro-category and region hierarchies (external mappings needed)
